About Kite

Kite are a Swedish synth-pop group composed of Nicklas Stenemo (The Mo, Melody Club) and Christian Berg (Yvonne, Strip Music, The April Tears). Originating from Malmö and now based in Stockholm, they are signed to Astronaut Recordings and have published six EPs and three full-length albums since their debut in 2008, each of which was released on CD and as a limited vinyl record edition. They have performed at festivals and also had some bigger concerts in Sweden and the nordic countries, Germany, USA, United Kingdom and China. The band has been described as a "modern version of the 1980s synth pop" and has been compared to the sound of Kraftwerk, Yazoo, and OMD.

Reception

Their eponymous debut EP Kite was reviewed favorably by the Side-Line magazine comparing it to classic 1980s synth-pop acts such as Erasure or Yazoo, while the German Sonic Seducer noted singer Stenemo's distinctive voice. The EP Kite III was lauded for its original sound by Side-Line and Sonic Seducer, the latter marking a darker tone in this release. The release Kite IV has been seen as a mix of classic 1980s synth-pop similar to OMD and modern, experimental sounds. Kite's sixth EP VI was released in 2015. It has been compared to the sound of Kraftwerk and Vangelis.
The band has received positive reviews from music critics. The Swedish newspaper Sydsvenskan wrote that the band "has a sound that is both modern and nostalgic, with catchy melodies and thoughtful lyrics." The newspaper also praised the band's live performances, calling them "energetic and engaging." The Swedish music magazine Sonic wrote that the band's music is "a perfect blend of retro and contemporary sounds, with a unique style that sets them apart from other synth-pop bands." The magazine also praised the band's songwriting, calling it "clever and insightful." Overall, Kite has been well-received by critics and fans alike, with many praising their unique sound and engaging live performances.

Since their inception in 2008, Kite has chosen to release an EP each year rather than traditional albums and singles. This has resulted in the band remaining somewhat in the shadows of radio and the media. Mainstream media rarely reviews anything other than full-length albums, while radio stations—both commercial and public—prefer singles that fit standard formats. It also led to that the band for a long time was extremely under-rated by many people. However, they have since 2020 released three full-length albums, which has led to a wider recognition of the band and their music.
And especially since the band has made a few spectacular live performances, such as the concert at the Royal Opera House in 2019 and recently "Kite on Ice" in 2025, which both was fast sold out. Kite on ice was also broadcasted on Swedish national television, the band has gained a wider recognition and a growing fan base.

Something about their sound

The band members of Kite are known for their use of vintage synthesizers, which they use to create their unique sound. They have a collection of synthesizers, including the Roland Juno-60, the Korg MS-20, the russian Polivoks and the Moog Minimoog. They also use a variety of effects pedals and other equipment to shape their sound. The band's music is characterized by its use of catchy melodies, driving rhythms, and lush synthesizer textures. They often incorporate elements of 1980s synth-pop, new wave, and electronic music into their songs, creating a sound that is both nostalgic and contemporary. Their music often features prominent synthesizer leads, arpeggiated basslines, and atmospheric pads, which contribute to the overall dreamy and ethereal quality of their sound.
