KITE

If you like synth-pop music, you will love Kite.
I accidentally discovered this band when their concert "Kite on Ice" was broadcasted on Swedish national television in 2025. I was immediately captivated by their unique sound and mesmerizing performances. Since then, I've been a devoted listener, eagerly following their music releases. Kite's music has a way of transporting me to another world, filled with emotion and energy. I highly recommend checking out Kite for yourself.
On this website, you can get information about Kite, view their gallery, and even purchase their merchandise.

The band members of Kite are known for their use of vintage synthesizers, here is one of them, the Roland Juno-60.
